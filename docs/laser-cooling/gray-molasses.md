## Theory

A type of sub-Doppler cooling that combines a polarization gradient, a Sisyphys cooling mechanism, and dark-state coupling.

Counterpropagating beams that have orthogonal linear polarizations will create a spatial polarization gradient that twists from linear to circular and back over a wavelength.
