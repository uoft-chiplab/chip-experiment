## Experiment

The optical pumping beam is comprised by Rb Trap + RP and K Trap + RP, combined on dichroics and fiber coupled. The output fiber is oriented along the lab's X direction. A difficult-to-reach QWP sets all the polarizations to $\sigma^+$, which is needed in order to optically pump Rb towards the stretched state $\ket{F=2, m_F=2}$ and K towards the stretched state $\ket{F=9/2, m_F=9/2}$.

Note that the Xbias coil needs to be on to provide the correct quantization axis for the OP light. We shine OP for usually around 200 us.

### Is OP working or not?

Do fluoresence imaging of the QMT and try to maximize counts. Note that even if OP does not work, some of the states will still be magnetically trappable (not only the stretched states). However, by reversing the Xbias field (easily possible because the power supply is bipolar), the $\sigma^+$ light becomes $\sigma^-$ in the atom frame and pumping goes to a magnetically nontrappable state. One can then look for minima in QMT fluoresence by scanning frequency and adjusting beam alignment.
